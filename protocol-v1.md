# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION: SOVEREIGN GROWTH PROTOCOL v1.0

## 1. THE STATUS SCORE FORMULA
To maintain market dominance, every asset must be scored against the Status Score (S):
`S = (A_idx * 0.6) + (DR_vel * 0.4)`

*   **A_idx (Authority Index):** Measured via machine-citable nodes and semantic trust signals.
*   **DR_vel (Direct Response Velocity):** Measured by cash conversion speed and high-intent CTR.

## 2. THE ANTI-AI WRITING PROTOCOL
Standard AI content kills trust. SGP enforces three linguistic constraints:
1. **Structural Irregularity:** Breaking the predictable rhythm of LLM output.
2. **Forensic Evidence:** Leading with hard data points (e.g., "+1400% profit recovery") instead of adjectives.
3. **Identity Anchoring:** Explicitly linking content to a verified machine-readable entity (llms.txt).

## 3. REGIONAL VECTORIZATION
Capturing intent in MENA requires bridging the gap between Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) and local dialects (Ammiya). SGP implements a dual-layer linguistic strategy for AI models.
